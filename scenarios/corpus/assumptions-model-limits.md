## What the simulator is
The simulator is a deterministic, hourly, simplified model of a fictional district. Runoff, drainage, overtopping, wind exposure and slope susceptibility use documented simple equations with uncalibrated parameters.

## What it is not
It is not a hydrodynamic, geotechnical or weather forecast model. Disruption values are demonstrative indicators. Sensitivity ranges come from seeded perturbations and are not calibrated probabilities.
