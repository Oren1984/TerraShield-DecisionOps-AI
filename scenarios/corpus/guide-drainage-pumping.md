## Outfalls at high sea level
Gravity outfalls stop discharging when the coastal water level rises above the outfall. During a surge the zone relies on pump stations, so ponding grows quickly once the outfall is blocked.

## Clearing drains
Clearing drains and outfall screens increases gravity drainage capacity. It is cheap and quick, and it is required before temporary pumps are connected, because temporary pumps discharge into the same network.

## Temporary pumps
A temporary pump adds self-powered capacity in a ponding zone. It does not depend on the grid, which makes it useful when a pump station loses power.
