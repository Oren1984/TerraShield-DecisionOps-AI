## Choosing between sandbags and barriers
Sandbag perimeters raise local protection by about 0.4 m and need only one crew for two hours. Demountable barriers raise protection by about 1.0 m but consume a barrier set and take three hours. Use barriers where expected water depth above floor level exceeds what sandbags can hold back.

## Priorities
Protect assets whose failure spreads to other assets first. Pump stations and substations come before single-purpose buildings, because losing pumping or power raises water levels or removes function elsewhere in the district.

## Lead time
Protection must be complete before water reaches the asset. Count back from the simulated hour of first flooding and include crew travel; work started after flooding begins does not count as protection.
