## Susceptibility index
The index multiplies a road slope's susceptibility by the square of soil saturation. It is a screening proxy for slope instability, not a prediction of landslides or earthquakes.

## Restriction levels
Above 0.35 the slope is on watch. Above 0.45 the ground role restricts the road as a precaution. Above 0.5 the simulator treats the road as closed. A restricted or closed slope road stays closed for the rest of the event.

## Slope drainage
Slope drainage lowers the index by about 40 percent but takes two crews four hours. It only helps if it is finished before the restriction level is reached.
