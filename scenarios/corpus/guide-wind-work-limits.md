## Working limits
Elevated work such as securing the telecom mast or bracing roofs stops above 12 to 15 m/s sustained site wind. Barrier and flood-wall installation stops above 18 m/s. Ground-level work continues up to about 25 m/s.

## When the window has closed
If wind already exceeds an action's limit before the action could finish, the action is infeasible for this event. Look for a substitute that does not need elevated work, for example a mobile communications unit deployed from the depot in place of mast securing.

## Exposed roads
Bridges and ridge roads close to traffic when gusts exceed their closure level. Routes that depend on them may disappear around the wind peak.
